# Community conduct

Discuss evidence and ideas respectfully. Welcome questions and corrections, credit contributions and keep feedback specific to the work. Harassment, threats, discriminatory attacks and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful public content and restrict participation where necessary. For a sensitive concern, use an available private contact channel for the repository owner; do not post private details publicly. No dedicated reporting team or response-time guarantee is claimed.

# Bank service assistance design

Primary pain: fragmented authorized information lookup and learning handling rules. First scenario: credit-card charge identification and apparent duplicate charges. The target process connects scoped read tools, evidence, applicable policies, reviewed notes/referrals and durable human continuation.

[Current contracts](../projects/bank-service-assist/specs/contracts/interface-notes.md) and [owner decisions](../projects/bank-service-assist/docs/sprint-01/decision-log.md) are authoritative. No automated financial execution. Voice and retail are later scope.

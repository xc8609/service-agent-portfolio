# Portfolio scope

This public repository connects BA requirements/process design, data analysis and software engineering around simulated service workflows. Bank service assistance is first; voice is its extension; retail clienteling follows separately. The design draws on customer-service desktop and banking product/BA experience without claiming former-employer sponsorship or measured results.

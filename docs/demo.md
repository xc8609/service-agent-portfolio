# Bank service workflow — a five-minute walkthrough

**Audience:** BA/BSA and developer reviewers. **Question:** “I bought once — why are
there two charges?” Show how a business problem became rules, software and test evidence.
[One-page case study](case-study.md).

**Demo setup:** synthetic records and business rules, with a scripted offline Agent
provider. The walkthrough shows the working integration and review flow.

**Recorded run:** a 96-second captioned recording of the same enquiry in the working
application ([GIF](media/bank-walkthrough.gif) · [MP4](media/bank-walkthrough.mp4)).
It is generated by a scripted browser session against fresh synthetic data;
[how to re-record it](../apps/web/demo/README.md).

## Read the case on GitHub

Start with the customer's statement:

> I bought once, but I see two charges for CAD 85. I cannot tell which entry is
> extra. I do not have the receipt.

The screenshots below show stages captured from the working application. Follow
the decisions and handoffs here; the local presentation script is available below.
This example needs investigation. If the evidence supports an answer and no further
work is needed, the representative can explain and save a contact note instead.
Agent assistance is optional in either path.

### 1. Establish what is known

The **representative** checks the authorized account and compares the two entries.
Both show CAD 85 at DEMO STORE. Matching values alone do not establish why both
entries exist. The customer's statement is saved separately, with recognition set
to **one purchase; entry mapping unresolved**. Staff can keep the pair together
for investigation without asking the customer to guess which entry is extra.

![Transaction comparison with currency, status and UTC dates](images/lookup-fixed-manual.png)

### 2. Find the applicable procedure and current owner

The **representative** checks existing work and handling rules in the same workspace.
The seeded case already has an investigation, so this contact can be linked to it.
The procedure provides its applicable conditions, source and version. These give
staff a basis for the next step and preserve a clear owner for the enquiry.

[See the handling-rule view](images/handling-rules.png).

### 3. Turn evidence into a reviewed handoff

The **Agent** organizes the selected evidence into **Facts / To confirm / Next step**
and prepares a note. The **representative** checks the sources, edits as needed and
approves the saved note and enquiry. The handoff carries the customer's statement,
the transaction pair and the missing-receipt gap. The **specialist** decides whether
additional material is needed; customer service owns contacting the customer.

![Saved customer statement alongside the Agent summary](images/lookup-fixed-handoff.png)

### 4. Investigate and prepare the explanation

The **specialist** reviews the enquiry, records findings and publishes the approved
customer explanation. Internal notes remain separate. In this example, further
action is still required, so the response explains the current position without
presenting the case as resolved.

### 5. Deliver feedback and check what remains

The **feedback representative** claims the task, verifies the customer's identity,
delivers the approved explanation and records the contact outcome. The screen then
shows **Feedback delivered · case remains open** with the outstanding work. Staff
can see what to follow up before confirming closure.

![Delivered feedback with outstanding work before closure](images/closure-checklist.png)

## What this demonstrates

- **Business analysis:** separate customer claims from source facts, define decision
  rights, allow missing material to follow, and make ownership and closure explicit.
- **Agent assistance:** bring evidence and a reviewable draft into the representative's
  workflow, keeping the original customer context visible.
- **Delivery and acceptance:** connect those choices to story criteria, implemented
  controls, observed issues and retests in the [case study](case-study.md) and
  [core acceptance record](uat/bank-core-closeout.md).

### 0:00–0:40 — Problem and scope

**Show:** the [case study](case-study.md) and transaction workspace.

> This project starts with a service-desk problem I understand from banking product
> and business-analysis work. A customer sees two similar charges, while the
> representative must find transaction details, check existing work and locate the
> right procedure. I designed one workspace to bring those steps together while
> preserving access restrictions. The goal is less searching and clearer handoffs.

### 0:40–1:30 — Establish facts before explaining

**Do:** **Verified inbound representative** → **Start demo contact**. Enter the statement
below *before* inspecting evidence. Search `DEMO STORE`, select both rows, then
**Inspect selected evidence (2)**.

> The representative can inspect only the authorized account. Both entries are posted
> for 85 Canadian dollars. The source does not record a relationship between them,
> so matching amounts do not prove a duplicate. The same workspace shows an existing
> enquiry, its progress and responsible queue. That gives staff an owned next step
> before another investigation is created.

### 1:30–2:20 — Explain the BA decisions

**Do:** choose **Recognizes one purchase; entry mapping unresolved** → **Save customer
report**. Show the rule's applicability, source version and suggested steps.

> I separated what the customer says from what the transaction source proves. If the
> customer cannot distinguish the entries, the process keeps them together rather
> than forcing a choice. A missing receipt is a recorded gap, not a reason to refuse
> the enquiry. The specialist decides whether it is needed; customer service owns
> the contact. Those decisions became story criteria, permission checks and tests.

### 2:20–3:20 — Show the Agent's actual contribution

**Do:** enter the question below → **Run scoped offline assistant**. Show **Facts /
To confirm / Next step** and **Sources and review checks**. In section 5: **Load note
history** → **Review saved note draft …** → review/edit → **Approve and commit note**.

> The assistant assembles permitted evidence into a draft staff can inspect and edit.
> It separates facts, uncertainty and next steps, with source references. The
> customer's original statement stays visible alongside the summary. Staff can
> check the evidence, edit the draft and approve the final note. Permissions and
> evidence checks run through the same application controls as the manual workflow.
> This makes the Agent useful at the point where staff need to organize what they
> know and decide what to do next.

### 3:20–4:15 — Show an accountable handoff

**Do:** section 6 → **Prepare fresh referral from selected pair**. Keep **Link existing
investigation**, add the material gap below, confirm the summarized concern, **Save
referral draft** → **Approve specialist enquiry**. Show the receipt. In section 7,
start **Demo specialist Alex** and open the `specialist_investigation` work.

> Approval links this contact to the existing enquiry. The specialist receives the
> customer's statement, selected transactions, unresolved recognition and missing
> material. A receipt confirms the handoff, and the responsible queue is visible.
> Staff can use the same workflow directly or start from the assistant's draft.

### 4:15–5:00 — Show follow-up and completion

**Show:** prepared feedback view or [closure screenshot](images/closure-checklist.png).

## Copy-ready synthetic inputs

| Field | Text |
|---|---|
| Customer statement | I bought once, but I see two charges for CAD 85. I cannot tell which entry is extra. I do not have the receipt. |
| Assistant question | What is known about these charges, and who should handle the next step? |
| Material gap | Purchase receipt unavailable; specialist to assess whether it is needed. |
| Specialist's approved explanation | The two posted entries have been reviewed. A required action is still outstanding. We will provide an approved update when it is available. |

**Prepare the feedback scene:** as specialist, select **Further action required** and
publish the explanation above. Start **Feedback representative**, claim its task and
open a synthetic callback. Save and approve the feedback review, simulate verified
callback identity, retrieve the verified disclosure, then record **Verified, reviewed
feedback delivered** within the service window. The seeded case contains pending
action/complaint work: show **Feedback delivered · case remains open** and its blockers.
Browser regression **WF-06** covers successful closure after all prerequisites are met.

Optional checks for a longer discussion: unclaimed feedback detail is denied; an
approved review without fresh callback verification still returns `AUTH_REQUIRED`.
Both were observed in the screenshot rehearsal.

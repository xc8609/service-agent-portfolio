## Claim labels

- **Owner-reported:** the owner described experience; no independent employer verification.
- **External evidence:** a dated publisher states the cited fact; adoption or benefit is not ours.
- **Design hypothesis:** a plausible pain, mechanism or outcome still needing observation.
- **Simulated rule:** a deliberately authored demo decision; not a bank/legal rule.
- **Measured:** reserved for an executed study/test with method, environment, denominator and evidence.

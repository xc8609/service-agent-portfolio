# BA-02 Analysis design

Version 0.1 · Draft. This designs a BA artifact, not completed application code.

## Rationale

Separate actual portfolio-owner authority from simulated banking roles. Exactly one accountable role per decision; proposal, approval, execution and acceptance are distinct. Record only the elicitation that actually occurred.

## Deliverables and interfaces

Simulated stakeholder/persona register, decision-right matrix, elicitation record and organizational-change needs.

Downstream contracts: BA-06 permission/owner rules, BA-07 auth/data contracts, BA-08 controls, QA-03 review and OPS-03 release authority.

Canonical business content: [spec](spec.md). Cross-story evidence, decisions and scenarios: [Sprint 1 index](../../docs/sprint-01/README.md). Changes require a CR and version update before dependent implementation. Avoid duplicate authoritative requirements in Trello; synchronize the review candidate by version.

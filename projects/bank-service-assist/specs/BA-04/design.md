# BA-04 Analysis design

## Rationale

Both manual and assisted evidence paths converge on human review. Complaint signal and operational task need are independent. Authoritative state and approved commit remain in domain services. Draft rules inform BA-06, not a premature final policy baseline.

Target workflow, representative/system step table, 16 draft rules, failure/recovery matrix and state distinctions.

Downstream contracts: BA-06–09 formalize rules/state/data/NFR/API; DEV-06/07/08 implement approval and fallback.

Canonical business content: [spec](spec.md). Cross-story evidence, decisions and scenarios: [Sprint 1 index](../../docs/sprint-01/README.md).
